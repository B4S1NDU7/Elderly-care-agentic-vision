Case 2: CAREGIVER INTERFERENCE (Expected Failure Mode)
  Description: When a caregiver enters the scene, the system may
               detect two people and misclassify the primary patient.
  Cause: YOLOv8 detects all persons; primary person selection uses
         highest confidence, which may not always be the patient.
  Impact: False state transitions during caregiver visits
  Mitigation: Person re-ID across frames (ReID models) or temporal
              consistency checking to track the primary subject.

Case 3: POOR LIGHTING / NIGHT MONITORING
  Description: In low-light conditions, VLM visual analysis degrades.
               The system may default to UNKNOWN or make errors.
  Cause: GPT-4o Vision quality degrades with noisy/dark frames.
         MediaPipe Pose also has reduced accuracy in poor lighting.
  Impact: Higher UNKNOWN rate, reduced classification accuracy.
  Mitigation: Night-vision preprocessing (histogram equalization,
              CLAHE), or use of infrared-aware models.
